# <Complex Waves>

## What this is
A complex wave usually consists of several waves at once or are created by modulating a standard wave.
- Was ist dieses Konzept?
- In einem Absatz, ohne Beispiele

## Why it matters in modular synthesis
While standard waves are having great potential to create several timbres, sometimes you want to create something new and therefore a standard wave is not sufficient.
