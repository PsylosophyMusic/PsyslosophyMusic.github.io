# Feedback

Feedback describes routing a signal back into itself or into an earlier stage of the signal path.

In modular systems, feedback can:
- emphasize certain frequencies
- introduce instability
- create complex or evolving behavior

Feedback often reacts non-linearly. Very small changes in level or routing can lead to significant sonic shifts.

## Why feedback matters
Feedback turns static structures into reactive systems. Instead of fixed results, the patch begins to respond to itself.
