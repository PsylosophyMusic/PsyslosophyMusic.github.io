# Oscillators

An oscillator is a signal source that generates a repeating waveform over time.

In modular synthesis, oscillators are commonly used as:
- sound sources
- modulation sources
- components in feedback systems

Not all oscillators are meant to be perfectly stable. In many cases, small instabilities are musically desirable and add movement or character.

## Why oscillators matter
Oscillators define the raw material of a patch. Everything that follows — filtering, modulation, dynamics — reacts to what the oscillator provides.

## Typical usage
- Oscillator → Filter → VCA
- Oscillator → FM input of another oscillator
- Oscillator as modulation source
